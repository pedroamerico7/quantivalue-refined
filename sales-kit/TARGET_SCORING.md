# Target Scoring

Score each company from 0–2 on each criterion.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand fit | Weak | Adjacent | Direct AI/finance/valuation fit |
| Ability to pay | Unknown/small | Funded/growing | Established or well-funded |
| Timing signal | None | General expansion | Recent funding, launch or rebrand |
| Decision access | No contact | Generic contact | Named founder/executive |
| Domain improvement | Strong current brand | Neutral | Current domain is limiting |

Priority:
- 8–10: A
- 5–7: B
- 0–4: C

Start with 25 Priority A targets. Do not begin with hundreds of unqualified companies.
