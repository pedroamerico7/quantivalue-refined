# QuantiValue Outbound Playbook

## Goal
Create qualified conversations with founders, CEOs, brand leaders and corporate-development teams that could use QuantiValue.com as an AI, finance, analytics or valuation brand.

## Initial cadence
- Day 1: personalized email
- Day 4: short follow-up
- Day 10: value-oriented follow-up with enterprise page
- Day 21: polite close-the-loop message

## Priority order
1. Funded AI-finance and analytics scale-ups
2. Valuation, private-markets and investment-intelligence companies
3. Enterprise software companies launching a finance/AI product
4. Venture studios and founders preparing a new company
5. Large strategic organizations, approached selectively

## Daily operating target
- Research 10 companies
- Contact 5 highly relevant decision-makers
- Send no generic bulk email
- Record every contact in the CRM
- Follow up on schedule

## Personalization rule
Every message must include one sentence explaining why QuantiValue fits that specific company. Do not claim an existing relationship or urgency that is not real.

## Qualification signals
High priority:
- Recently funded
- Hiring in AI, finance, data or brand
- Launching a new product
- Rebranding or expanding internationally
- Weak, long or country-specific current domain

## CTA
Use a low-friction invitation:
“Would it be useful if I sent the short executive brief?”
