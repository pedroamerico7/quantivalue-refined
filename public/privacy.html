Privacy

Private inquiry information.

This page explains how information submitted through the QuantiValue.com acquisition form is used.

Information collected

The form may collect your name, company, position, country, business email, proposed amount, message, IP address, country inferred from network metadata and browser user agent.

Purpose

Information is used solely to review, qualify and respond to potential acquisition inquiries concerning QuantiValue.com.

Storage and disclosure

Inquiry information is stored privately using Cloudflare infrastructure. It is not displayed publicly or sold for advertising purposes. Information may be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to complete a mutually agreed transaction.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while an inquiry is under review and for a reasonable period afterward for transaction, security and recordkeeping purposes. A deletion request may be sent to sales@quantivalue.com.

Privacy and acquisition questions: sales@quantivalue.com
